# ⭐ Exam-ready answer

### Q. What were the causes of the struggle of 1857?

The major causes were:

1. **Changes in the village system:** The British changed the existing village system, creating feelings of instability and insecurity among people.

2. **Economic causes:** New revenue systems burdened farmers. British policies damaged Indian handicrafts and textile industries, resulting in unemployment among artisans.

3. **Social causes:** Indians felt that the British were interfering in their culture, traditions and customs. Social reforms such as the prohibition of Sati and the Widow Remarriage Act were viewed by some Indians as interference in their lifestyle.

4. **Political causes:** The British annexed many Indian states. Awadh was annexed on the grounds of maladministration, while Satara, Nagpur and Jhansi were annexed through the Doctrine of Lapse. This created distrust towards the British.

5. **Discontent among Indian soldiers:** Indian soldiers received lower status, lower pay and reduced allowances. They also could not rise above the rank of Subhedar.

6. **Immediate cause:** The introduction of Enfield rifle cartridges created anger among Indian sepoys because they were reported to be greased with cow and pig fat, hurting the religious sentiments of Hindu and Muslim soldiers.
